My claim was rejected months ago. Can it still be challenged?

Often, yes. Each stage has time limits, so share your documents as soon as you can and we’ll check where you stand.

Do you guarantee my claim will be approved?

No one can honestly guarantee that. The final decision rests with the insurer or the Ombudsman. What we promise is an expert, well-prepared case and steady follow-up.

Can I approach the insurer or Ombudsman myself?

Yes. Policyholders can approach the insurer’s grievance cell, the IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal and the Insurance Ombudsman directly, free of cost. We help people who want experienced support through that process.

What documents do I need?

Usually your policy copy, the rejection or settlement letter, the discharge summary, hospital bills and any letters exchanged with the insurer. Send what you have and we’ll tell you if anything else is needed.

Which insurers do you work with?

We help with claims from public and private health insurers and TPAs across India.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your documents are used only to work on your case and are never shared without your consent.
