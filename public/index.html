If the insurer still says no

India has a set path for insurance complaints. We take your case up it, one step at a time.

  1. The insurer’s grievance cell

    A written complaint to the insurer, which must acknowledge it within 3 working days and resolve it within 14 days.

  2. Bima Bharosa, IRDAI’s complaint portal

    The regulator’s portal records the complaint and tracks it with the insurer.

  3. Insurance Ombudsman

    Free, for claims up to ₹50 lakh, once the insurer has rejected your complaint or not replied within a month. Apply within a year.

  4. Consumer commission

    When the Ombudsman route is not open to you, or the case needs to go further.

Based on IRDAI rules and the Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017. We check which steps and deadlines apply to your case.

My claim was rejected months ago. Can it still be challenged?

Often, yes. Each stage has time limits, so share your documents as soon as you can and we’ll check where you stand.

What do you charge?

The first review of your case is free. If we take it up, our fee is 10 to 20% of the amount the insurer pays you, agreed in writing before we start. You pay it only after the settlement reaches you.

Do you guarantee my claim will be approved?

No one can honestly guarantee that. The final decision rests with the insurer or the Ombudsman. What we promise is an expert, well-prepared case and steady follow-up.

Can I approach the insurer or Ombudsman myself?

Yes. Policyholders can approach the insurer’s grievance cell, the IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal and the Insurance Ombudsman directly, free of cost. We help people who want experienced support through that process.

What documents do I need?

Usually your policy copy, the rejection or settlement letter, the discharge summary, hospital bills and any letters exchanged with the insurer. Send what you have and we’ll tell you if anything else is needed.

The insurer is sitting on my claim. Is there a deadline?

Yes. Once an insurer has all the documents it needs, it must settle within the time IRDAI sets, and a late settlement carries interest at 2% above the bank rate. We chase the claim and escalate when the deadline passes.

Which insurers do you work with?

We help with claims from public and private health insurers and TPAs across India.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your documents are used only to work on your case and are never shared without your consent.
