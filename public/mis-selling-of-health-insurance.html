Sold the wrong health insurance policy?

In short: Mis-selling is when a health policy is sold with false promises or hidden facts, for example an agent filling in your health details without asking you, or a fixed-benefit plan sold as full hospital cover. A new policy can be returned within the free-look period. After that, complain to the insurer in writing, then to Bima Bharosa and the Insurance Ombudsman.

Signs your policy was mis-sold

  • The agent filled in the proposal form or your health details without asking you. This often leads to a non-disclosure rejection later.
  • You were promised “no waiting period” or “everything covered from day one”.
  • A fixed-benefit or critical illness plan was sold as hospitalisation cover.
  • A top-up or super top-up plan was sold as your main cover.
  • The policy was pushed on you with a loan or another product.
  • The premium, sum insured or room-rent limit differs from what you were told.

What to do, step by step

  1. Compare promises with the policy. Read the policy schedule and wording against what you were told.
  2. Keep your proof. Messages, emails, brochures, call recordings, and the agent’s name and licence details.
  3. Use the free-look period. For a new policy, you can return it within the free-look period, now 30 days under IRDAI’s 2024 rules, and get a refund minus small deductions.
  4. Complain in writing. Write to the insurer’s grievance cell with your evidence.
  5. Escalate. Go to Bima Bharosa, and then the Insurance Ombudsman, which also hears complaints about misrepresented policy terms.

If your proposal form has wrong health details, talk to us before you file a claim. Fixing it after a claim is much harder than before.

Questions people ask

What is the free-look period?

It is the time after you receive a new policy during which you can return it if it does not suit you. Under IRDAI’s 2024 rules it is 30 days, and you get your premium back minus small deductions such as stamp duty and medical test costs.

The agent filled my health details wrongly. What now?

Tell the insurer in writing and ask for the proposal form to be corrected. Doing this before a claim protects you from a non-disclosure rejection later. Talk to us first if you are unsure.

Can I complain about mis-selling after the free-look period?

Yes. Complain to the insurer’s grievance cell, then Bima Bharosa and the Insurance Ombudsman if needed.

Is a critical illness plan the same as health insurance?

No. A critical illness plan pays a fixed amount when a listed illness is diagnosed. It does not pay your hospital bills the way a regular health policy does.
